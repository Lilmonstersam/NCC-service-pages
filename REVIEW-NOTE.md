27 October 2026

The mockup now uses Namoli's existing wording as its baseline. I restored the original hero copy, body paragraphs, list items, calls to action, three existing FAQs, page titles and descriptions on the office, industrial, warehouse, school and childcare pages. Each added FAQ uses facts already stated on its live page. The original three FAQ questions and answers remain intact on each restored service page. The additional questions cover competitor themes found in the Ahrefs review: quote process for office cleaning, equipment for industrial cleaning, warehouse zone coverage, term-break scheduling for schools and quality monitoring for childcare. The revised headings make the service topic explicit without replacing the page's main message. FAQ structured data now matches the four visible questions on each page.

The earlier Ahrefs review recommended these primary terms: **commercial cleaning services** for the overview, **office cleaning brisbane**, **industrial cleaning services**, **warehouse cleaning services**, **school cleaning services** and **childcare cleaning services**. Its Australian SERP analysis found that leading service pages address scope, scheduling and the way work is managed. These edits cover those gaps using Namoli's existing claims. Search volumes and competitor rankings are dated snapshots, not promises of ranking improvement.
